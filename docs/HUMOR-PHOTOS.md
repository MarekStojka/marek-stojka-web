## Přesné zadání

Use case: product-mockup. Create a realistic minimalist studio photographic study for a Czech author's humorous books. Reference only for the real wood pencil shavings and pigment colors, NOT as a collage. Landscape 3:2. A single plain white clothbound hardback book stands at a relaxed slight angle, on a white seamless tabletop, right half of the composition. Blank white cover, cream page edges visible, no title or lettering. Its base is tucked into a small loose heap of REAL curled wooden colored-pencil shavings with bright magenta, yellow, cyan and cobalt pigment edges, visibly natural wood fiber and tiny pigment crumbs. Soft beautiful daylight, tactile materials, gentle contact shadows. Broad white negative space on the left. Lively and slightly cheeky product photography, restrained museum-quality composition, not cartoon or CGI, no floating magical objects, no additional props, no text or logos, no watermark. This is clearly a blank prototype book, not a final published cover.
