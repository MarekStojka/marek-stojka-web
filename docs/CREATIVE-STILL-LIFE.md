# Zátiší pro bílý magazín o kreativitě

Dvě nové AI fotografické studie vytvořené vestavěným ImageGen 5. 11. 2026. Nejde o Markovy vlastní fotografie. Originály zachované v generated_images, projekt používá kopie níže a Astro je optimalizuje do WebP.

- `src/assets/creative-still-life/banan-zatky-v1.png` — pod titulkem představení magazínu.
- `src/assets/creative-still-life/lahev-vlastovka-v1.png` — oddělení workshopů a osobního příběhu.

Obě fotografie 1536 × 1024, bílý ateliér, přirozené barvy předmětů a měkké kontaktní stíny. Bez barevného překryvu. Nepoužívat jako nové obrázky sedmi rubrik; papírová série zůstává.

## Přesné prompty

### Banán a zátky

Create a photorealistic editorial still life for a minimalist Czech creativity magazine website. Seamless pure white studio floor and background (#ffffff), soft large studio light from upper left, delicate real contact shadows. One real ripe yellow banana curves like a smile. Exactly three small unbranded metal beer bottle crown caps balance improbably but physically plausibly along the banana's upper curved edge, like passengers. Surprising, understated, thoughtful, beautifully composed rather than goofy clipart. Landscape 3:2 composition, centered small still life occupies about 45 percent of width, abundant whitespace. Real banana pores and brown speckles, metal texture, realistic photographic detail, neutral white balance. No text, no labels, no logos, no collage, no frame, no graphics, no colored backdrop. This will visually separate prose sections, photograph not illustration.

### Lahev a vlaštovka

Create a photorealistic editorial still life for the same minimalist creativity magazine. Seamless pure white studio floor and background (#ffffff), soft large studio light from upper left, delicate natural contact shadows, neutral white balance. One small empty clear glass beer bottle with no label stands upright. A single folded white paper airplane balances horizontally on its mouth as if the bottle is a launch pedestal. One unbranded metal crown cap lies nearby. Everyday objects combined in an unexpected but elegant, plausible physical composition. Landscape 3:2 image, centered arrangement occupies roughly 40 percent of width and 65 percent of height, generous empty white space. Real glass reflections, subtle paper creases, photographic material detail. No liquid splash, no typography, no logos, no people, no illustration, no colored background, no frame.
