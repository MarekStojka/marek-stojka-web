## Prompt — momentka

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit this real photograph of Marek for his personal website. His exact face and identity are the highest priority: preserve facial proportions, eyes, hairline, facial hair, natural skin texture, expression and body shape. No beautification, no slimming, no face substitution, no new pose, no AI-smooth skin. Photographic retouch only. This is the main candid desk photo. Keep his real relaxed sideways glance, folded hands, black illustrated T-shirt, ring, desk and laptop, the blue butterfly in the foreground and wooden wall slats. Improve natural exposure and white balance: bright soft window daylight, neutral-warm skin, restrained slightly desaturated colors, deep natural blacks, gentle film-like texture. Keep butterfly blue as a restrained accent. Crop to a refined vertical 4:5 editorial photograph, removing most of the empty upper wall while keeping the full head, shoulders, folded hands and recognizable butterfly in frame. Simplify distracting background: remove wall light switch and visible light stand at right, retain a believable softly lit real room. Do not invent decor or props. No text or overlays.
