## Přesný prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create one photorealistic editorial still-life photograph for a minimalist Czech creativity magazine website, portrait 4:5 composition. On a pure seamless white studio floor and pure white background (#ffffff), a small plain clear glass bud vase holds one sharpened purple wooden pencil standing upright like a flower stem. A single loosely crumpled ball of ordinary white paper is physically attached to the upper end of the pencil, suggesting a simple handmade flower. It must look like a real arrangement that can easily be made and photographed: no floating parts, no impossible balance, no illustration. The glass vase is narrow and low enough that a generous length of the pencil remains visible above it; the pencil and paper flower are the only things inside. Entire arrangement centered, occupies about 65 percent of frame height and 45 percent of frame width, generous clean white margins on every side. Crisp photographic material detail, neutral white balance, quiet playful idea. No leaves, no real flowers, no additional props, no text, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no coloured background.
